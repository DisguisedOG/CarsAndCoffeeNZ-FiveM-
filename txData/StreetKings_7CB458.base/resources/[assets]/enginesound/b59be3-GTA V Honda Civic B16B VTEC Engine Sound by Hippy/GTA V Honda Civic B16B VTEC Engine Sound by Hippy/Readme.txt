Description :
This mod is made for this Honda Civic Type-R (EK9) car mod by YCA-y97y : https://www.gta5-mods.com/vehicles/honda-civic-type-r-ek9
But you can use it on any car by changing <audioNameHash /> to <audioNameHash>ISSI2</audioNameHash> in the appropriate vehicle.meta.
Because this mod replaces the Issi's engine sound, some other cars using the same engine sound will sound similar.
The original files are included.
I hope you'll enjoy it !

Installation :
- Extract "GTA V Honda Civic B16B VTEC Engine Sound by Hippy.zip" anywhere you want
- In the mod's files, you can listen to the samples ("Samples" folder) to know how the car is going to sound like
- Put hatchback_1_eur_4_cyl.awc in Grand Theft Auto V\(mods)\x64\audio\sfx\STREAMED_VEHICLES_GRANULAR.rpf\
- Put hatchback_1_eur_4_cyl_npc.awc in Grand Theft Auto V\(mods)\x64\audio\sfx\STREAMED_VEHICLES_GRANULAR_NPC.rpf\
- Go in Grand Theft Auto V\(mods)\update\update.rpf\dlc_patch\spupgrade\common\data\levels\gta5\ and extract the vehicle.meta anywhere you want
- Open the vehicle.meta you extracted and search for "blista2" (Ctrl + F to search)
- Then look for the line that says <audioNameHash /> and change it to <audioNameHash>ISSI2</audioNameHash>
- Save the file, and put it back into OpenIV in the same location
- The mod is installed ! You can launch your game, and enjoy your new B16B VTEC Engine Sound !
